CREATIVE WORKSHOP & EVENT DIRECTORY

Football Only Expo 2026

A one-day fan art expo and creative workshop series for football fans. Draw, craft and watch for the game you love.

Workshops and Activities

Fan Art Sketch Lab

Details
Draw your favourite player in action. Bring a photo on your phone, and our volunteers will help you turn it into a simple line sketch you can colour in.

Register: Fan Art Sketch Lab

Saturday, Dec 5 · 11:00 AM · Room A. Pencils and paper are provided. Free with your expo ticket.

Team Bracelet Making

Details
Braid a beaded bracelet in your club's colours, add a player's number and take it home. Every seat comes with a full bead kit.

Register: Team Bracelet Making

Saturday, Dec 5 · 12:30 PM · Room B. Seats are limited to 30 fans. Free with your expo ticket.

Football Film Screening

Details
Watch and discuss iconic football films and farewell moments, from legendary careers to the tifos fans built to say goodbye.

Register: Football Film Screening

Saturday, Dec 5 · 2:00 PM · Main Hall. Bring tissues for the farewell clips. Free with your expo ticket.

Fan Market and Swap

Details
Shop the fan booths for handmade zines, cards, badges and stickers, or bring your own creations to trade with other fans.

Register: Fan Market and Swap

Saturday, Dec 5 · 10:00 AM to 5:00 PM · Market Hall. Bring cash or items to swap. Free with your expo ticket.

Signature Wall

Details
Leave your name and a message for your club on the giant Football Only wall, then take a photo with it as a souvenir.

Register: Signature Wall

Open all day · Entrance lobby. Markers are provided. Free with your expo ticket.

Fan Stage Show

Details
Fans take the stage to lead chants, wave club flags and show off their mascot costumes. Sign up to perform or just come to cheer.

Register: Fan Stage Show

Saturday, Dec 5 · 4:00 PM · Main Hall stage. Performers check in at the info desk by 3:30 PM. Free with your expo ticket.
